问答 · 第一句直接回答

客户到底是谁？怎么找到？
A chatbot platform: a company that runs support bots for thousands of businesses, in markets where people switch alphabets, such as Russian, Hebrew or Arabic. One integration covers every business on it, and at their volume 1 in 10,000 is thousands of messages a day. One such platform already built a layout fix itself, so they know the problem. I reach them through introductions from this room.
平台为什么愿意付钱？
Three reasons. Volume: a platform with 100 million messages a day sees about 10,000 of these a day. Reach: one integration covers every business on the platform. Cost: it lets them stay on cheaper models, which understood 46 of 107, instead of paying for the best model on all traffic. 单个银行每月只有几十次，付费理由弱；平台量大、卖一次覆盖全部客户。还没有任何平台确认过，这是六个月试点要验证的。
“Billions” 是怎么来的？愿景是不是太大？
China alone has over a billion people online, and most type Chinese through Latin letters. Add Hindi, Arabic, Russian, Korean, Japanese. Wrong keyboard is where I start, because I can measure it. The layer is the same for all of them. 愿景是方向，不是已经做到的事。今天做到的只有键盘还原和拼音反问两样。
这两天你改了什么？
Three things. The customer: from the person typing to the platform that runs the assistant. The claim: I counted before claiming, and found it is rare per message and large in total. The plan: one paid pilot with one platform, instead of a product for everyone.
六个月的目标现实吗？
It is small on purpose: one platform, one count on their own data, one fixed-fee pilot. The tool that does the count already exists.
大模型不是已经解决了吗？
For the commonest case, mostly: 90 of 107, and it still asked or misread one time in six. The cheaper model got 46, and sometimes guessed wrong with confidence. And “beizi” no model can settle without asking.
数据从哪来？用了什么数据集？
Three sources, all public. The test questions are from Belebele, Meta’s multilingual reading benchmark; I retyped the same questions as wrong-keyboard text. The real messages are from WildChat, released by the Allen Institute for AI: conversations between real users and a free ChatGPT-based service, April 2023 to July 2025. And the 107 cases I replayed last night are real messages from those logs. 逐个数字对应：7 和 51 → Belebele 四选一阅读题，每种语言 96 题，俄语、希伯来语、亚美尼亚语、阿拉伯语等。49% 和 99% → 我自己出的 72 道拼音题，词表来自 CC-CEDICT 词典和词频表。万分之一 → WildChat，530 万条。90 和 46 → WildChat 里核实过的 107 条。80 亿条/天 → seoClarity 和 Similarweb 的公开估计。追问隐私：WildChat 是用户同意后公开发布的，我只用公开版本。
这是旧数据，现在呢？
The open logs run to July 2025; nobody publishes this year’s. The rate is the same order. GPT-4o got 18 of 74 real cases. For this year’s models I replayed them last night: 90 and 46.
万分之一，太小了吧？
Per message, yes. But AI assistants now handle about 8 billion messages a day; at 1 in 10,000 that is about 800,000 messages a day. At the upper bound of what I measured, 2.5 in 10,000, it is 2 million a day. In Russian conversations it is about 1 in 2,000, and these are lower bounds. I am extrapolating from one service, which is why the first step is a count on one platform’s own data. 算法：ChatGPT 每天约 45 亿条（seoClarity 2026 年 10 月估计：OpenAI 2025 年 7 月公布每周 180 亿条、7 亿周活，2026 年 9 月周活 12 亿，按人均不变推算）。ChatGPT 约占 AI 助手访问量的 55%（Similarweb 2026 年 8 到 9 月），所以全部助手约 45 ÷ 0.55 ≈ 80 亿条/天。× 万分之一 = 每天约 80 万条。上限：第一段数据的区间上限是每万条 2.5，80 亿 × 2.5/10,000 = 每天 200 万条。被追问来源时照这个顺序说。5.3 million 是我数过的样本量，被问“你数了多少”时再说。两段数据分别是每万条 1.0（2023–24，样本小）和 0.7（2024–25，样本大），合起来约 0.8，对应每天约 64 万条；区间低端每万条 0.4 对应约 30 万条。所以屏幕小字写的范围是 30 万到 200 万。被追问“为什么取 1”时答：“The two periods give 0.7 and 1.0; I rounded to 1 in 10,000. On the pooled rate it is about 640,000 a day. Either way it is hundreds of thousands a day.”
这很容易抄吧？壁垒在哪？
The code is easy. Anyone here could write it in a week. What nobody had until this week is the measurement: how often this happens and which models fail. That is what I own, and the prize buys the next one.
大厂明天自己做了怎么办？
Then it is solved for their users, and my benchmark is how everyone checks it. I would count that as a good outcome.
怎么实现的？延迟多少？
A plain program, no neural network: about 800 kilobytes of code and lookup tables. It replays the key positions on twenty keyboard layouts and scores each reading with character statistics. Under a millisecond for a short message, a few milliseconds for a long one, on a laptop.
原型能找回那 51 分里的多少？
I don’t have an end-to-end number. 51 is with the correct text supplied. Measuring what the prototype recovers, counting wrong repairs, is the second thing the prize pays for.
原型在真实消息上准吗？
On an independent sample of real messages it restored 10 of 13. One went to the wrong layout; two very short ones were missed. About 1 in 2,000 other messages is still wrongly flagged, mostly code. Run on every message today, wrong flags would outnumber real cases about five to one. So the layer keeps the original text next to the repair, and cutting false flags is the first thing the pilot measures.
第 2 页说修复只找回 7 分，第 4 页说旗舰读懂 90 条？
Different measures. Slide 2 is answer accuracy on test questions in my registered study, reasoning off. Slide 4 is whether the reply addressed what the person meant, on real messages, mostly Russian. The study has reasoning off and covers layouts beyond Russian, such as Armenian, where models do much worse. 先核对登记实验这一项用的语言再说后半句。不要说两者一致。
beizi 为什么上限是 50%？
Each question is asked twice. The model sees “beizi” and four options: lifetime, cup, quilt, plate. Once the writer meant cup; once, quilt. The two questions look exactly the same, so whatever the model answers, it is right at most one time in two. Models scored 48.9: they are at that limit. The information is not in the text. beizi 有三个读法，但只考其中两个（杯子、被子）：同一道题出两次，标准答案不同，所以上限是 50%，不是 33%。共 18 组 × 2 = 36 题，只给拼音，必须选一个。句子里的 99.1% 用的是另外 36 个词，例如 “gongsi … gei meiwei gudong fenhong”（股东，不是古董或鼓动）。
own transcription 和 correct text 是什么？
Same questions, three ways. One: the model gets the wrong-layout text and answers. Two: it first transcribes the text itself, then answers from its own transcription: plus 7 points. Three: we hand it the correct text: plus 51. So it can answer. What it cannot do reliably, with reasoning off, is read. +6.7 = 模型自己先还原再答；+51.4 = 直接给它正确原文。差的 45 分就是“读”这一步丢的。
谈过客户吗？谁付钱？
No, not yet, and nobody pays yet. The buyer I am testing is a chatbot platform in a two-alphabet market. First milestone: one paid pilot with one platform, at a fixed fee.
那 256 条是谁核实的？
Each case was reviewed one by one, with a written reason, by an AI reviewer under strict rules, and a sample was checked again. The review files are kept. 你自己看过样本，才加 “I read a sample myself”。
“反问”也算没读懂？
Asking is better than guessing, but the person has to type it again. I report them separately: in the first set, 72 asked and 17 misread.
有人是故意这么打的吗？
Some are. One user in 2025 wrote, in wrong-layout Russian: I have no Russian layout, correct the text and read it as Russian. The assistant asked what they meant.
Claude 呢？
I replayed them on GPT-6.2 last night. Claude is running now; I don’t have that number yet.
奖金怎么花？
Three things: the first audit of one platform’s logs, an end-to-end test of the prototype, and a public leaderboard of the gap.
不知道怎么答时
I don’t know yet; that is what the audit answers.
